## 6. Near-me + Gemini readiness

Near-me AI answers lean on:

1. Organic ranking of the **city** (or model×city) landing  
2. Strong GBP + reviews in that geo  
3. Quotable on-page FAQ / intro (already shipped on-site)  
4. Consistent entity (Organization / AutoDealer schema)

**Retest monthly** (screenshots): Used Creta near me, Used Creta in Palanpur, 2–3 other inventoriable model×city queries, 2 consultant names. Log % where Reecomm is cited — see `docs/SEO_KPI_TRACKING.md`.

## 7. What ops will not do

- Promise overnight displacement of CarWale / OLX / CarDekho / Spinny on every Gemini card  
- Create empty doorway pages for cities with zero inventory  
- Fake LocalBusiness addresses or review spam  

Off-site + inventory density decide **how often** you win; on-site makes every live car and consultant **able** to compete.
